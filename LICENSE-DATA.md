## What is not ours to license

The package quotes short excerpts of developer-authored privacy policies and
Google Play Data Safety declarations for the purpose of research analysis and
verification. Copyright in those excerpts remains with their authors; the CC BY
licence above covers our selection, annotation and arrangement, not the
underlying documents.

The audit judgments record whether one disclosure artifact is supported by
another. They are research annotations, not legal determinations of compliance
and not allegations of wrongdoing by any developer. We ask that reuse naming
individual applications preserve that framing.
